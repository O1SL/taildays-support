Information we process

Local pet records, diary editing, local photo tools and the pet-sound tool use device storage. Camera, microphone, photo and location permissions are requested for the feature you choose. The sound tool processes recordings on your device. The widget uses a local shared snapshot and thumbnails.

For online features, Sign in with Apple provides an account identifier. The current sign-in request does not ask Apple for your name or email. We use account and session records to authenticate requests and associate online tasks with the right account.

When you choose online AI creation, our service processes the selected images, creation instructions, task status and generated results. Apple processes payments; we process verified transaction, subscription and wallet records to deliver and restore benefits. We do not ask you to enter payment-card details in TailDays.

Service logs and security records may include request references, status, timing, errors and network-derived identifiers. If you contact support, we process the information you choose to send. App diagnostic exports are optional.

AI services and processing locations

Before sharing selected content with third-party AI services, TailDays must clearly explain that processing and obtain your permission. Permission for a disclosed AI service can cover later creations within that scope; it does not authorize uploading your entire photo library.

Our business backend is hosted in the United States. The current image-creation service is Volcengine Ark / Seedream, provided by Beijing Volcano Engine Technology Co., Ltd. and applicable affiliates, using a mainland China service. The video service is the MiniMax international platform, provided by Nanonoble Pte. Ltd. MiniMax's privacy policy describes storage in United States data centers and possible international transfers. A backend location is not a promise that every provider operation occurs in that country.

Providers process content under the terms applicable to the service used, including service operation, safety and permitted service or model improvement. Their retention and independent legal requirements may differ from ours. See the Volcengine model terms, Ark plan terms, MiniMax Privacy Policy and MiniMax Terms of Service.

Family care and maps

When you invite or join a care relationship, Apple's CloudKit private and shared databases exchange supported records, original photos and videos, and complete GPS routes from finished walks with members who have access. A route in progress is not published through this shared-care path. Maps use Apple MapKit.

Our service stores account-linked invitation-code mappings. CloudKit also processes invitation policy, joining and relationship-status metadata separately. Leaving a relationship stops future sharing but cannot remove copies another member has independently retained.

Retention and account deletion

Account and service records are kept for the features you use, delivery, purchase handling, support and security. Uploaded and generated media are temporary service files rather than permanent cloud storage. They are expired and cleaned up according to task and delivery needs. Download creations you want to keep. Copies saved on your device or in your iCloud remain subject to your controls.

You can request account deletion through the account controls available in your version or by contacting support. We verify ownership and process the request. A request still being processed is not completed deletion. Completing deletion includes removing account-linked user information and recoverable copies under our control, including applicable backups. We do not keep optional copies for reactivation, analytics or marketing after completion.

Apple's independent transaction records, copies independently held by other care members, and provider-held data are subject to their respective controls and terms. Any records retained because of a legal obligation are limited to that requirement. Account deletion does not itself cancel an Apple subscription; use Apple subscription settings.

Your choices and contact

You can change device permissions in iOS Settings, stop submitting new AI tasks, and use available account or sharing controls. To request access, correction or deletion, or to withdraw permission for future AI processing, contact junlin.wu@outlook.com. Withdrawal cannot undo processing already completed by an independent provider.

This first release does not include advertising. We use access controls and HTTPS to protect service data. TailDays is not directed to children under 13; contact us if you believe a child's personal data was submitted. We will update this policy when processing changes and seek new permission where required.

处理哪些资料

宠物记录、手帐编辑、本机照片工具和声音玩具使用设备存储。相机、麦克风、照片及位置权限按你选择的功能请求。声音玩具在本机处理录音，桌面组件使用本机共享快照和缩略图。

在线功能使用通过Apple登录获得的账号标识，当前登录请求不向Apple索取姓名或邮箱。服务使用账号和会话记录验证请求，将任务关联到正确账号。在线AI创作会处理你选择的图片、创作说明、任务状态及生成结果。付款由Apple处理，我们处理已验证的交易、订阅和钱包记录，用于交付和恢复权益，不要求你在TailDays输入银行卡资料。

服务日志和安全记录可能包括请求参考、状态、耗时、错误及网络来源标识。联系支持时，我们处理你主动提供的信息；导出App诊断资料是可选操作。

第三方AI与处理地区

向第三方AI分享内容前，TailDays须明确说明处理方式并取得授权。已说明范围内的授权可以覆盖后续创作，不代表允许上传整个相册。

业务后端部署在美国。当前图片服务使用北京火山引擎科技有限公司及适用关联方提供的火山方舟／Seedream，涉及中国大陆服务；视频使用Nanonoble Pte. Ltd.提供的MiniMax国际平台。MiniMax隐私政策说明资料存储于美国数据中心，并可能发生跨境传输。业务服务器所在地不代表供应商所有处理都发生在该地。

供应商按实际服务条款处理内容，包括服务运行、安全及条款允许的服务或模型改进；其留存和独立法律要求可能与本服务不同。具体见上方链接的火山模型及套餐条款、MiniMax隐私政策和服务条款。

共养与地图

发起或加入共养后，Apple CloudKit私有和共享数据库向有权限的成员交换支持的记录、照片和视频原件、已完成遛遛的完整GPS路线；进行中的路线不会经该共养路径共享。地图使用Apple MapKit。业务服务保存账号关联的邀请码映射；CloudKit另处理邀请策略、加入和关系状态元数据。退出关系停止后续共享，但不能删除其他成员自行保留的副本。

保留和删除

账号和服务记录用于你使用的功能、交付、购买处理、支持和安全。上传及生成的媒体是临时服务文件，不是永久云存储，按任务及交付需要到期清理。需要留存的作品请及时下载；保存在本机或本人iCloud中的副本由你控制。

可通过当前版本提供的账号入口或支持邮箱申请注销。核验归属后处理请求，处理中不等于已完成删除。完成注销包括删除本服务控制的账号关联用户资料和可恢复副本，涉及备份也纳入处理；完成后不为重新激活、分析或营销保留可选副本。

Apple独立交易记录、其他共养成员自行保留的副本及供应商持有资料，按其各自控制方式与条款处理。因法律义务保留的记录仅限相应要求。注销不自动取消Apple订阅，请在Apple账号设置中管理。

选择与联系

可在iOS设置调整权限、停止提交新的AI任务，并使用已有账号或共享控制。访问、更正、删除或撤回未来AI处理授权，请联系上述邮箱。撤回不能逆转独立供应商已经完成的处理。首版不接广告；服务使用访问控制和HTTPS保护资料。App不面向13岁以下儿童，发现儿童个人资料被提交可联系我们。处理方式变化时会更新政策，并在需要时重新取得授权。
